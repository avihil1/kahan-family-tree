# Jozsef Moldovan Katz parentage research

Identify the father and mother of **Burek Jozsef Moldovan Katz**, also recorded as Joszep, Joszip, Joszif and under Moldovan, Katz and Kahan. His WikiTree birth around 1790 and death around 1837 are estimates. **Neither parent is identified by the evidence examined so far.** Updated 13 October 2026.

## Original census evidence

**Slom Moldovan, Bréb, 1848:** household 1 records age **60**, country of birth **Moldavia**, an unknown birthplace town, and **55 years** of residence both in Hungary and in Bréb. Arrival around **1793**, at roughly five years old, is an inference from those two recorded figures. His wife Toba is 35, from Barczanfalva; children include Cill, Eszter Malka, Golda, Liber and Volf. The census records a Moldavian birthplace for Slom; Jozsef’s birthplace remains unestablished.

[Original image 17, first household](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS84-X9QJ-8). Film 719825, image group 007953483.

**Leb Moldovan, Gyulafalva, 1848:** household 6 records age 22, born in Bréb, a wife aged 22 and son Mojse aged one. This supports the branch identified on WikiTree as Jozsef and Rifke’s son Chaim Arye Leb.

## Jozsef’s household

The profile’s citations place Joszep Katz in Brébb in 1813–1814 and Joszip Moldovan there in 1821–1822 with a wife, three sons and one daughter. Birth records name wife Tsarna in 1824 and 1826. Tsarna died on 21 February 1826, aged 38. Subsequent births name Rifke, including Hajim on 24 June 1827; later entries use Ilona or Ilona/Rifke. A 1838 tax entry describes the widow of Joszip Katz. These records establish family context but do not name Jozsef’s parents.

[WikiTree profile and citations](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Moldovan_Katz-1).

## Gaps and identity conflicts

The indexed death entries from Bréb Register 1271 jump from 1826 to 1863. Jozsef’s estimated death falls within that gap. This describes the index coverage; it does not establish that the original register lacks the record.

Slom’s original census age of 60 in 1848 conflicts with the 1864 death index age of 105. Keep the identities unresolved. Similar surnames, neighboring households and repeated given names alone do not establish parentage.

A separate death record for **Abraham Moldovan**, aged 85, on **10 December 1896**, names parents **Lazar Moldovan and Czarna Gancz** and wife Nyicza Anna Markovics. It identifies a real couple for further comparison, but neither their equivalence to the 1795 Lazar household nor their relationship to Jozsef is established. The original has now been inspected: official catalog **MM-F-00743-1-1-00288**, page **60**, entry **89**. The earlier index-derived reference used Fund 753; see the original-record details below.

[JewishGen Hungary database](https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/Hungary/).

## Gravestones and original records: additional findings, 13 October 2026

**Jozsef’s father and mother remain unidentified.** This pass recovered cemetery photographs, checked burial records,
and inspected an original Romanian-held death register. It establishes the parent names recorded for Abraham

### Original record now inspected: Abraham Moldovan

The original Hungarian-language civil death entry identifies **Moldován Ábrahám**, an Israelite day laborer, born and
resident in Bréb, aged 85, who died there on **10 December 1896**. Registration was on 11 December. His wife is recorded
as Markovics Nyicza Anna. The parent fields read:

- Father: **“néhai Moldován Lázár”** — the late Lazar Moldovan; day laborer; Bréb.
- Mother: **“néhai Gancz Czárna”** — the late Czarna Gancz; household occupation; Bréb.

There is high confidence that these names are what the original record states. As information supplied at an adult’s
death, the parentage still merits corroboration in an earlier family record. The recorded age implies birth around
1811, not an independently established birth date.

**Source:** Romanian National Archives, Maramureș, **MM-F-00743-1-1-00288**, Ocna Șugatag deaths 1895–1899,
folio/page **60**, entry **89**. PDF **MM-FD-00743-1-1-0288_040.pdf**, PDF page **22**, right-hand entry.
The current official catalog uses **00743**; the earlier JewishGen-derived citation in our ledger used **753**.
