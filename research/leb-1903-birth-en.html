Reading notes and exact source

This is a research translation, prepared 14 October 2026. Square brackets identify explanations, blank fields, and uncertain readings. The father's final given-name letter is ambiguous; JewishGen transcribes it as Bernad. Names and historical place names are retained rather than translated.

“The child's lawful father” translates the printed heading A gyermek törvényes atyja. This heading is not a separate marriage record. “Assisting family member” is the mother's recorded occupational description; it is not a statement about another named relative.

Birth: 10 April. Registration: 16 April. These are different events. The original names both parents directly but gives no grandparents, and does not give the child's later Hebrew double name.
