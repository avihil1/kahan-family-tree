with source-family coverage, visited identities, a resumable frontier and per-person change summaries.
The naming guidance distinguishes a common Ashkenazi custom from an absolute rule. It also guards against
inferring death from remarriage, a birthplace from residence, or parentage from shared surnames.

### Dov / Jente / Lipot: first evidence checkpoint, 15 October 2026

**New relationship evidence, not a newly discovered birth:** the authenticated Máramaros births search reproduces
Jente, 7 February 1894, father KAHAN Bernat and mother LIEBERMAN Rifke, both Gyulafalu; Book 1101, 37-01.
Lipot’s [photographed Holocaust family memorial](https://images.findagrave.com/photos/2023/176/131335147_24c45f38-da7d-4ceb-9ec4-dab019c3aa0f.jpeg)

daughter of Reb Berl. The father is Yaakov, son of Liber. The family is commemorated as murdered in Auschwitz in
1944. This materially supports the existing Jente-to-Bernat/Dov Ber connection, together with the birth index and
Be’er Chaim memorial already in the baseline. The linked [Geni Jente](https://www.geni.com/people/Jente-Lieberman/6000000016589578823)
instead names Avraham as father. That contrary tree assertion is preserved; no original naming Avraham was found
in this check. The father’s given name Berl alone would not identify a person without the connected household.
The memorial’s collective mention of murdered siblings cannot mean every sibling died: Naftali survived.
Do not identify unnamed siblings or assign individual death places from that collective sentence alone.

**New descendants:** Lipot’s [memorial 131335147](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/131335147/lipot-lieberman)
links wife Chaja Ganz (1924–2011), son Jack Samuel (1946–1996) and daughter Pearl Jessica (died 2026).
